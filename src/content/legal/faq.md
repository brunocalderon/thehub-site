## What is Tratto?

Tratto is an independent magazine about design. We write about type, interfaces and identity, and about the studios that make them.

## How often do you publish?

We publish new articles every weekday and send a newsletter every Friday with the best of the week.

## What do members get?

Members read every article, including members only features, and get full access to the archive. Membership also keeps Tratto independent and free of advertising cookies.

## Can I cancel my membership?

Yes, at any time from your account page. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

## Do you offer student or team plans?

Yes. Students get half price membership with a valid student email. Teams of five or more can write to [support@tratto.test](#_) for a team plan.

## Can I pitch an article?

We welcome pitches from designers and writers. Send a short summary and two samples of your writing to [pitches@tratto.test](#_).

## How do I advertise?

We offer a small number of sponsorships each month. See the advertise page for details.

## Who do I contact for anything else?

Write to [support@tratto.test](#_) and a real person will answer within two working days.
