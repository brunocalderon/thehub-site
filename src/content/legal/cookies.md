## What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. They help the site remember who you are between visits.

## The cookies we use

- **Essential cookies** keep you signed in and remember your membership. The site does not work without them.
- **Preference cookies** remember choices such as newsletter settings.
- **Analytics cookies** count visits and popular articles. They do not identify you personally.

We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not let advertisers place cookies on Tratto.

## Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block essential cookies, you will not be able to sign in.
